# Translation Decisions: copyright.html

## Overview
This is the copyright/legal page. It contains no AWS or software engineering terminology — only publishing roles, legal disclaimers, and edition information.

| Term | Translation | Rationale |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Acquisitions Editor | 策划编辑 | Standard Chinese publishing industry role title |
| Development Editor | 开发编辑 | Standard Chinese publishing industry role title |
| Production Editor | 制作编辑 | Standard Chinese publishing industry role title |
| Copyeditor | 文字编辑 | Standard Chinese publishing industry role title |

## Structural Decisions
- All HTML attributes, class names, and comments preserved unchanged.
- All proper nouns (person names, company names, URLs, ISBNs, phone numbers) kept in original form.
- "by" (author attribution) rendered as "作者：" following Chinese book conventions.
- HTML comments kept verbatim as they are production notes not intended for readers.
