# Translation Decisions: ch21 — Designing an Online Stock-Trading Platform

## Overview
This chapter covers the design of an online stock-trading (stockbroker) platform, focusing on real-time stock tick delivery, order management systems, P&L dashboards, ultra-low-latency (HFT) considerations, and AWS deployment architectures.

## Structural Decisions

1. **Chapter title**: "Designing an Online Stock-Trading Platform" → "设计在线股票交易平台"
   - "在线" is natural for "online" in Chinese tech context
   - "股票交易平台" is the standard Chinese term for a stock-trading platform

2. **Figure captions**: All translated to Chinese to match the localized document

3. **Section headings**: All translated to natural Chinese, preserving the original structure

4. **Code/equations**: DynamoDB schema elements (Partition key, Sort key) translated to Chinese with explanatory labels

5. **Company names (Zerodha, Robinhood, Groww)**: Kept as English proper nouns; no established Chinese names

6. **Exchange names**: NYSE→纽约证券交易所, NSE→印度国家证券交易所, Nasdaq→纳斯达克; all standard Chinese names per zh.wikipedia.org

7. **"The system should operate at submillisecond (<100 ms) latency"**: Note that "submillisecond" technically means <1ms; in context the author means "sub-100ms" (explicitly stated). Translation uses "亚毫秒级别（< 100 ms）以内" to faithfully preserve both the term and the explicit number.

8. **"Day 0 architecture" / "Day N architecture"**: Kept as "Day 0 架构" / "Day N 架构" per established glossary entries #381 and #382 (non-official but recognized industry terms)

9. **biryani**: Kept as "biryani（香料饭）" with a parenthetical Chinese explanation; this is a proper noun food name from Indian cuisine

10. **Cloudflare CDN**: Kept as "Cloudflare CDN" — proper noun; no Chinese name

## Ambiguity and Notable Choices

- **"order"** (stock trading) vs "order" (general): Used "委托订单" or "委托" in stock trading contexts, and "订单" in more general data/system contexts. This distinction is maintained consistently throughout.

- **"hot cache"**: Translated as "热缓存" (consistent with established pattern of "hot partition" = 热分区 in glossary).

- **"proof of concept (PoC)"**: Translated as "概念验证（PoC）" — standard Chinese tech term.

- **"bare-metal EC2 instances"**: Translated as "裸金属 EC2 实例" — consistent with glossary entry #621.

- **"tombstones" (Cassandra)**: Translated as "墓碑" — consistent with prior chapter translations.

- **"prewarm"** (infrastructure): Translated as "预热" — natural Chinese IT term for warming up infrastructure.

- **"autoscaling"**: Translated as "自动扩缩" — consistent with AWS official Chinese documentation.

- **"gray rollout / percentage-based rollout"**: Translated as "百分比灰度发布策略" — "灰度发布" is the standard Chinese term for gradual/canary deployment.
