# Translation Decisions: colo.html

## Overview
This is the colophon page — a brief publisher's note about the cover animal, illustration credits, and typefaces used in the book. It contains no AWS or software engineering terminology.

## Key Translation Decisions

| Term | Translation | Rationale |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| Colophon (section title) | 出版说明 | Standard Chinese rendering for O'Reilly colophon pages; "版权页" refers specifically to copyright, while "出版说明" better captures production notes |
| IUCN | 国际自然保护联盟（IUCN） | Official Chinese name on first mention; abbreviation retained |
| Least Concern | 无危（Least Concern） | Official IUCN Chinese classification; English retained in parentheses for clarity |
| western spectre dragonfly | 西方幽灵蜻蜓 | Direct translation of common name; scientific name *Boyeria irene* kept unchanged |
| *Histoire Naturelle* | 《自然史》（*Histoire Naturelle*） | Standard Chinese title for this classic French natural history work; French original retained |
| "all of them are important to the world" | 它们对世界来说都弥足珍贵 | "弥足珍贵" (invaluable/precious) chosen over the literal "重要" to convey the tone of the original; slightly elevated register matches the closing sentiment |
| "eye-catching" | 引人注目的 | Standard Chinese equivalent |

## Structural Decisions
- Two source paragraphs merged into two target paragraphs; paragraph boundaries preserved.
- All proper nouns (person names, font names) kept in original Latin form as is conventional in Chinese publishing.
