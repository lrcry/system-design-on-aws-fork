## Key Translation Decisions

### 1. "URL frontier" → "URL调度器（URL Frontier）"
- The Chinese technical community uses multiple translations: "URL边界"（literal), "URL前沿"（literal), "URL调度器"（functional). We chose "URL调度器（URL Frontier）" as it best captures the functional meaning — the component that schedules/determines which URLs to crawl next. The English term is kept in parentheses for clarity.

### 2. "politeness" → "爬取礼貌性（Politeness）"
- In the web crawler context, "politeness" means respecting rate limits on web servers. The technical Chinese literature uses "礼貌爬取" or "爬取礼貌性." We use "爬取礼貌性" with the English in parentheses to avoid ambiguity.

### 3. Algorithm names (DRUM, STAR, BEAST, Simhash, IRLBot) kept largely in English
- These are proper algorithm/system names without established Chinese translations. We keep the English acronym and provide a parenthetical Chinese description for DRUM, STAR, and BEAST (which have spelled-out English names) on first mention. Simhash and IRLBot are left as-is since they are proper nouns.

### 4. "barrels" → "桶（barrels）"
- The Google architecture paper uses "barrels" as a data structure name. We translate it as "桶" (the standard Chinese CS term for bucket/container) with the English in parentheses for the first occurrence.

### 5. "hits" (partial forward index) → "命中（hits）"
- In the original Google paper, "hits" refers to partially sorted forward index entries recording word occurrences. We use "命中（hits）" as a translation, keeping the English in parentheses since it's a technical term from the 1997 Google paper.

### 6. "lexicon" → "词典（lexicon）"
- The term "lexicon" in this context refers specifically to an in-memory hash table mapping words to word IDs. We translate it as "词典" (lexicon/dictionary in Chinese NLP terminology).

### 7. "doc ID" / "word ID" → "文档ID（doc ID）" / "词语ID（word ID）"
- These identifiers are kept partially in English since they are used programmatically.

### 8. "content relevance system" → "内容相关性系统"
- A straightforward translation. "内容相关性" (content relevance) is the standard Chinese CS term.

### 9. "chaos engineering" → "混沌工程"
- This is the established official translation confirmed in AWS China blog posts (aws.amazon.com/cn/blogs/china/).

### 10. "AWS Fault Injection Service (FIS)" → "AWS故障注入服务（FIS）"
- Based on the meaning of "fault injection" → "故障注入," combined with AWS's Chinese naming pattern. The service is referred to as "AWS FIS" after first introduction, consistent with how AWS service short forms are handled throughout the book.

### 11. "refresh intervals" → "刷新间隔"
- Standard translation for Elasticsearch refresh interval setting. Confirmed consistent with OpenSearch Chinese documentation patterns.

### 12. "document-based partitioning" / "term-based partitioning" → "基于文档的分区" / "基于词项的分区"
- Literal, precise technical translations. "词项" is the standard Chinese translation for "term" in information retrieval contexts.

### 13. "Make It Work, Make It Right, Make It Fast" (referenced from ch14)
- Where cross-chapter references appear, we maintain the previously established translation: "先让它跑起来，再让它做对，最后让它快起来."

### 14. Numerical presentation
- "1.13 billion" → "11.3亿" and "200 million" → "2亿" for natural Chinese number representation (Chinese uses 亿 for 100 million).

### 15. Proper nouns retained in English
- Service names: Elasticsearch, Apache Lucene, Redis, Mercator, IRLBot, Dynamo, ZLIB — kept in original English as these are proper nouns widely recognized in the Chinese technical community.
- Google-internal components (URL server, store server, indexer, URL resolver, sorter, searcher) — translated into Chinese for readability since these are conceptual descriptions rather than product names.

### 16. "seed URLs" → "种子URL"
- "种子" (seed) is the established Chinese term in web crawler literature. "URL" is kept in its English abbreviation since it is universally recognized.

### 17. Chapter cross-references
- All `<a data-type="xref">` references preserved exactly as in the original HTML.

### 18. "nonfunctional requirements (NFRs)" → "非功能性需求（NFR）"
- Following the established translation from term #369 in the glossary.

### 19. "multipart upload and download" → "分段上传和下载"
- Confirmed as the official Chinese translation from docs.amazonaws.cn/AmazonS3.

### 20. Code block content
- The code-like listing showing inverted index structure was partially translated (the comment "词项（键）" replacing "Term (key)" and "文档ID，频率" replacing "docId, frequency") while preserving the actual programmatic values like `doc1`, `doc2`, etc.
