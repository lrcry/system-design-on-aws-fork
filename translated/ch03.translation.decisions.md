# Translation Decisions: ch03 — Nonrelational Stores


## General Principles (inherited from previous chapters)

1. **Preserve all HTML structure**: Tags, IDs, `data-type` attributes, `<a contenteditable>` indexterm anchors, cross-reference `href` values, and image `src` paths are kept verbatim.
2. **Term introduction**: On first occurrence, a key term is rendered as `<em>中文术语</em>` mirroring the source `<em>` tag. The English acronym is retained in parentheses where disambiguation is needed (e.g., `最后写入者获胜（LWW）`).
3. **Subsequent mentions**: Chinese only, no parenthetical repetition.
4. **Code/query-language keywords**: `GetItem`, `PutItem`, `UpdateItem`, `DeleteItem`, `CQL`, `Cypher`, `Gremlin` and similar API/query-language tokens are left unchanged.
5. **Brand/product names kept in English**: Apache Cassandra, MongoDB, Neo4j, Apache TinkerPop, Riak, Voldemort, Dynomite — consistent with ch02's treatment of MySQL/PostgreSQL/MariaDB as untranslated brand names.
6. **Chapter/section cross-references**: `<a data-type="xref">` inner text kept as-is.
7. **Critical near-synonym distinctions**: Several ch03 terms are easily confused in Chinese translation and require careful, consistent rendering across the chapter (see "Critical Distinctions" section below), following the rigor established by ch08's 编配(choreography) vs. 编排(orchestration) precedent.

## Critical Distinctions

These terms are easy to conflate in Chinese and require consistent, deliberate handling across the chapter, following the rigor of ch08's 编配 vs. 编排 precedent:

1. **"quorum" vs. "consensus"**: "quorum" (法定人数/quorum) refers to the minimum number of replica acknowledgments required for a single read/write operation to succeed; "consensus protocols" (共识协议, from ch01) refers to the broader agreement mechanism (e.g., Raft/Paxos) used to decide on a single value across nodes. Do not conflate 法定人数 with 共识 — ch03's quorum discussion is about per-operation replica thresholds, not full consensus.
2. **"optimistic replication" vs. "eventual consistency"**: Optimistic replication (乐观复制) is the *mechanism* (write locally, propagate asynchronously, assume success); eventual consistency (最终一致性) is the *consistency guarantee/outcome* that results from such mechanisms. The chapter uses them in a cause-and-effect relationship — translation must preserve this rather than treating them as synonyms.
3. **"sloppy quorum" vs. "quorum"**: Sloppy quorum (宽松仲裁) is a *relaxation* of the standard quorum (法定人数) requirement — it allows non-designated replicas to count toward W/R. Translation must keep 宽松仲裁 as a distinct, named technique rather than rendering it generically as "宽松的法定人数" inconsistently across mentions (pick one rendering and use it consistently — see entry above).
4. **"primary key" vs. "partition key" vs. "sort key" vs. "clustering key"**: In the key-value section, primary key (主键) may be composed of partition key (分区键) + sort key (排序键). In the columnar section, the composite primary key (复合主键) is composed of partition key (分区键) + clustering key (聚类键) — note "sort key" and "clustering key" are different English terms for conceptually similar-but-distinct features in different store types, and must NOT be translated identically. Keep 排序键 for key-value/DynamoDB-style stores and 聚类键 for columnar/Cassandra-style stores, per each section's own terminology.
5. **"memtable" vs. "commit log" vs. "SSTable"**: Three distinct Cassandra/columnar-store storage-engine components with a specific data flow (write → commit log for durability + memtable for in-memory buffering → periodic flush to SSTable on disk). Keep commit log as 提交日志, memtable in English, SSTable in English-with-gloss — do not merge or use overlapping Chinese phrasing (e.g., avoid using 日志 generically for more than the commit log).
6. **"compaction" vs. "(data) compression"**: As noted above, 压实/压缩合并 (compaction, merging SSTables) must be kept distinct from 数据压缩 (data compression, reducing storage footprint via encoding), since both concepts appear in the same Data Model section of the chapter.
7. **"replica set" (document stores) vs. "replication factor" (columnar stores) vs. "quorum" (key-value/columnar consistency)**: These are three different replication-related concepts tied to specific database families in this chapter (MongoDB replica sets 副本集; Cassandra replication factor 复制因子; tunable consistency quorum 法定人数). Keep them terminologically separate even though all relate to "how many copies of data exist/must respond."
