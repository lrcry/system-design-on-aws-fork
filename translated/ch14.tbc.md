- **Priority:** High (reviewer approved)
- **Question:** With decimal units, 31.53 billion × 1 KB = approximately 31.53 TB, not 29.37 TB. The value 29.37 corresponds approximately to TiB when the total bytes are interpreted using binary units. Can the English equation be corrected to 31.53 TB, retaining decimal KB/TB conventions?
- **Status:** Open — English-source correction required; do not edit the English file without explicit permission.
- **Reviewer decision:** Explicit paragraph-specific correction overrides the P58 Retain AS-IS selection. Chinese P58 is approved to read “一年的总存储量 = 315.3 亿 × 1 KB = 31.53 TB”. Reviewer prefers decimal TB rather than switching to TiB. This is a deliberate correction of the source's numerical error in Chinese, not a faithful reproduction of the English figure, and must remain flagged until the English source is resolved.
- **Relevant source:** Chapter 14 English P58; decimal SI prefixes (KB = 10³ bytes, TB = 10¹² bytes) and binary IEC prefixes (KiB = 2¹⁰ bytes, TiB = 2⁴⁰ bytes).
