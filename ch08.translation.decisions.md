# Translation Decisions: ch08 — Architectural Designs and Patterns

## General Principles (inherited from previous chapters)

1. **Preserve all HTML structure**: Tags, IDs, `data-type` attributes, `<a contenteditable>` indexterm anchors, cross-reference `href` values, and image `src` paths are kept verbatim.
2. **Term introduction**: On first occurrence, a key term is rendered as `<em>中文术语</em>` mirroring the source `<em>` tag. The English acronym is retained in parentheses where disambiguation is needed.
3. **Subsequent mentions**: Chinese only, no parenthetical repetition (unless a section restart makes it helpful for readers).
4. **Code elements**: `INSERT`, `UPDATE`, `DELETE`, `acks=all`, `PaymentProcessed`, `BookingConfirmed` and similar code tokens left unchanged inside `<code>` tags.
5. **Chapter/section cross-references**: `<a data-type="xref">` inner text kept as-is; the publishing system resolves these automatically.

## Notable Translation Decisions

### 1. 编配 vs. 编排 (choreography vs. orchestration)
This is the most critical distinction in this chapter. AWS Prescriptive Guidance Saga pattern documentation explicitly uses:
- **编配** for choreography (decentralized, event-driven)
- **编排** for orchestration (centralized, coordinator-driven)

Swapping these terms would reverse the meaning of multiple sections. Both terms were verified against the AWS Prescriptive Guidance docs at:
- https://docs.aws.amazon.com/zh_cn/prescriptive-guidance/latest/cloud-design-patterns/saga-choreography.html
- https://docs.aws.amazon.com/zh_cn/prescriptive-guidance/latest/cloud-design-patterns/saga-orchestration.html

### 2. Lambda 架构 (Lambda Architecture)
Capital "L" retained throughout to distinguish from AWS Lambda (the serverless function service). Chinese readers familiar with both will understand the distinction from context, and capital L reinforces it.

### 3. 变更数据捕获 vs. 更改数据捕获
AWS China blogs predominantly use **变更数据捕获**. AWS Prescriptive Guidance uses **更改数据捕获**. We standardized on **变更数据捕获** as it appears more frequently in AWS China content aimed at practitioners.

### 4. NameNode / DataNode bilingual rendering
On first occurrence: **NameNode（名称节点）** and **DataNode（数据节点）**. Subsequent mentions: English only. The English proper nouns are the canonical identifiers used in all Chinese Hadoop documentation and community content.

### 5. Kafka "Broker" kept in English
In Chinese Kafka documentation and community usage, "Broker" is universally retained in English. Translating it to 代理 or 中间人 would make the text harder to match with official Kafka documentation.

### 6. Schema Registry kept in English
A proper noun for a specific Confluent/Kafka component. No established Chinese name exists.

### 7. Section title "Conclusion" → "结语"
Preferred over 结论 (conclusion of an argument) as 结语 (closing remarks) better fits a chapter summary context.

### 8. "5 Vs of data" retained as abbreviation
"数据的五大挑战——数据量（Volume）、速度（Velocity）、多样性（Variety）、真实性（Veracity）和价值（Value）" — expanded in Chinese with English terms parenthetical, as the "5 Vs" framework is commonly referenced this way in Chinese big data literature.
