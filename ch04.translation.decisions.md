## 6. Stylistic Choices

- **Bilingual parenthetical form** used on first occurrence of key technical terms where the English form is widely recognised by Chinese technical readers (e.g., 缓存击穿（Cache Stampede）, 生存时间（TTL）). Subsequent occurrences use Chinese only.
- **Number formatting**: Arabic numerals kept as-is (e.g., P999, 24 小时).
- **Figure captions**: Translated as complete sentences in natural Chinese; do not add periods unless the original has them.
- **Table headers**: Translated directly; column order unchanged.
- **Footnotes / callouts**: Translated as body text; no structural changes.
